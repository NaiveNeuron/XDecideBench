# XDecideBench

XDecideBench is a benchmark of typed decisions across languages. A typed decision is a probability distribution over a declared, finite output space: one of *k* options, yes/no, or an ordinal level. The benchmark scores the submitted distributions, not generated text, on nine suites in 122 languages, and checks whether confidence policies fitted in English hold in other languages.

## Citation

Marek Šuppa and Jaroslav Kopčan. *XDecideBench: Evaluating Typed Decision Models and Confidence Policies Across Languages.* 2026. arXiv preprint.
